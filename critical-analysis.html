Critical Analysis: Five Case Studies

Five conversation-analytic case studies from my MA at the University of Queensland - three written papers and two video presentations. Choose one below.

Critical Analysis 1

Critical Analysis 1: Repairing a Misunderstanding in Conversation

A conversation-analytic study of how a misunderstanding in everyday talk is surfaced and repaired - and what that reveals about how intersubjectivity is maintained.

Written paper • University of Queensland • 2023

Critical Analysis 2

Critical Analysis 2: Requests, Offers and Storytelling in Everyday Talk

A video presentation analysing pre-requests, informative answers, counter-offers and storytelling sequences in naturally occurring conversation.

Video presentation • University of Queensland • 2023

Critical Analysis 3

Critical Analysis 3: Complaints and Blame in Troubles-Telling

A conversation-analytic study of how complaints and blame are constructed and responded to in troubles-telling.

Written paper • University of Queensland • 2023

Critical Analysis 4

Critical Analysis 4: Code-Switching and Identity in Greetings

A video presentation analysing greeting sequences, code-switching and member categorisation - how speakers construct identity and manage relationships in talk.

Video presentation • University of Queensland • 2023

Critical Analysis 5

Critical Analysis 5: Face, Politeness and Declining Invitations

A conversation-analytic study of how invitations are declined - face, politeness and the interactional work of refusing without offence.

Written paper • University of Queensland • 2023

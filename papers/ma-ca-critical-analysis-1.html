Critical Analysis 1: Repairing a Misunderstanding in Conversation

Abstract

A close analysis of how a misunderstanding between two colleagues unfolds and gets repaired - through clarification requests, accounts, tag questions and pauses - until mutual understanding is restored.

The Data

1 DAV: morning Rita, 2 RIT: hi David, how was your weekend? 3 DAV: I had some friends over, (0.1) we had a great time (0.2) 4 what about you? 5 RIT: I’m fine, I met up with Leesa and Mauro yesterday. 6 DAV: Mauro? 7 RIT: ye:s, Leesa’s cousin (0.2) he ca- 8 DAV: I know who Mauro is (0.3) but wasn’t he on a field trip? 9 RIT: he came back on Saturday after two: months in Indonesia. 10 DAV: ah I see (0.3) did you go to the Riverside café? 11 RIT: yea:h we did. an- and we ordered churros, 12 DAV: whAt? 13 RIT: a Spanish [dessert, 14 DAV: [no: how could you do that without me? 15 RIT: = I didn’t think you wanted to try them here (0.2) you were 16 talking about going to Spain next summer, weren’t you? 17 DAV: er yes (0.2) they will be much better in Spain 18 RIT: = yeah 19 (1.3) 20 DAV: are you going to go to the office meeting today? 21 RIT: when is it, 22 DAV: 2 pm 23 RIT: yes I can come at 2 pm. 24 DAV: cool.

David initiates the conversation, which serves as a generic pre-expansion to establish the opening of the conversation with a greeting sequence (Kendrick et al., 2020). Rita responds to the greeting and a question about David's weekend, this is an example of a preferred second pair part in a typical social exchange (Kendrick et al., 2020). In line 3, David gives an extended turn, which serves to provide more information in response. The pause indicates a transition from one idea to the next as David is expected to acknowledge and show interest in Rita's weekend, which he does in line 4, as a signal for Rita to take a turn in the conversation to maintain a social relationship (Kendrick et al., 2020).

In line 5, Rita initiates another sequence by providing information about her weekend. David requests clarification about Mauro (insert-expansion), indicating there is a misalignment during the interaction, this is an example of open-class other-initiated repair (Robles, 2017). Rita tries to resolve the misunderstanding, in line 7, using an account to explain who Mauro is, but David cuts her off with an acknowledgment of knowing who Mauro is. However, the pause, in line 8, indicates a transition and realisation of his behaviour, and he uses self-initiated repair, by asking for clarification to rectify the misunderstanding (Robles, 2017). This post-expansion allows Rita, in line 9, to finish the sentence, which David cut off in line 7, resolving the misunderstanding (Kendrick et al., 2020).

In line 10, David uses topic-shift by asking a new question about Rita's activities, which is a specific pre-expansion (Kendrick et al., 2020). Rita responds by confirming that she did visit the café, however, "yea:h" and her speech being interrupted by a self-repair in the form of a false start in line 11, indicates hesitation and uncertainty as she did not include David (Dingemanse& Floyd, 2014).

In line 12, David initiates an open-class repair sequence by asking for clarification about Rita's previous utterance, as "whAt?" can be interpreted as an assessment that expresses disbelief, which signals an inappropriate prior turn by Rita (Kendrick et al.,2020). Rita responds with other-initiated/self-repair by using an account to describe churros as a Spanish dessert, this is an assessment of dispreferred response by David, in line 14, because it does not match the expected or preferred answer (Kendrick et al.,2020). David shows an emotional account by expressing disappointment that Rita did not invite him to try the churros, which is a display of affiliation (Robles, 2013).

In lines 15-16, the cross-linguistic occurrence of tag questions allows Rita to provide an account to explain why she did not invite David, here Rita is asking for confirmation and show her knowledge of David’s interests, trying to repair the conversation, which is a pursuit of misunderstandings and other-initiated repair (Robles, 2017). In line 17, where David confirms going to Spain next summer where the churros are better. Rita responds with "yeah" which could be interpreted as confirmation, but potentially to show that they have achieved mutual understanding (Dingemanse& Floyd, 2014).

In line 19, there is a long pause, then David initiates a new sequence in line 20, and the conversation shifts to the office meeting topic, demonstrating the flexibility and adaptability of conversation to different topics and contexts (Dingemanse& Floyd, 2014).

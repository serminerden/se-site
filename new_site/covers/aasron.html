Meaning, Logic, and Quantification

Abstract

This paper explores how humans construct, interpret, and communicate meaning, bridging the domains of semantics, logic, and quantification. Part 1 introduces building blocks of language—how words, syntax, and context combine to create meaning. Part 2 analyses the conditional "if" and its representation in formal logic. Part 3 investigates universal and existential quantifiers.
